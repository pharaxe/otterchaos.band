Otter Chaos! at Furality Ultra

Thursday, June 4, 2026, 10:00PM CDT, VRChat

Key and Rhubarb are back for another live Otter Chaos! concert in virtual reality. Join them for violin and piano covers of some of your favorite video game tunes from the era of the UltraVox Prism-featuring tracks from Katamari Damacy, Super Mario Galaxy, Persona 3, and more!

How to attend

Register for Furality Ultra

The concert is open to all attendees of Furality Ultra. The General Pass is $35 which will get you access to the VRChat instances where all the events happen.
Age verification is required this year so make sure to read the requirements here: https://furality.org/ageverification.

Furality Registration

Watch the Twitch Stream

If you can't attend in VRChat, you can watch the show on twitch.tv/furality on Thursday, June 4, 2026, 10:00PM CDT.
