Friday, July 3, 2026, 3:00pm, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Otter Chaos! is bringing live video game music to Anthrocon 2026. Join us Friday afternoon for an energetic set featuring our full band of 15 furry musicians! We'll be performing original arrangements from games like Pokemon, Sonic, Metal Gear Solid, Final Fantasy, and Castlevania; featuring instruments such as accordion, electric violin, steel pans, and more!

How to attend

Register for Anthrocon

The concert is open to all Anthrocon attendees. On-site price for a badge is $100. There's also day passes available for $50.

Registration line can be long

Picking up your badge on Thursday or Friday morning is useful so you can make it to the concert on time.
