Otter Chaos! at Midwest FurFest

Friday, December 5, 2025, 5:00pm-6:30pm, Rosemont, Illinois
Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Apple Calendar (.ics)

Otter Chaos! is a group of furry musicians who love video game music. Their live concert at Midwest Furfest will feature boss battle music and villain themes from video games, performed by a wild ensemble of instruments including: accordion, steelpans, electric violin, theremin and more!

Don't miss the biggest Otter Chaos! concert yet. Add it to your schedule on the MFF 2025 Sched app.

How to attend

Register for MFF

The concert is open to all attendees of the convention. Badge price at the door is $55 for a Friday badge, or $110 for the full weekend badge.

Reg line can be long

Pick up your badge Thursday evening or Friday morning to make sure you can attend the concert.

MFF Registration Info

Getting there

  • Public transit: take the CTA Blue Line to the Rosemont stop, then transfer to the Rosemont Circulator bus which will bring you to the convention center.
  • Public parking: Parkway Bank Parking Garage is $15 a day.
  • Badge pickup: Hall A in the Donald E. Stephens Convention Center.
  • Concert: 5:00pm - 6:30pm on Friday Dec. 5th, Grand Ballroom (Main Stage) in the Hyatt Regency O'Hare.
