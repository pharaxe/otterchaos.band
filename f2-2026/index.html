Otter Chaos! at FurSquared

Sunday, February 8, 2026, 12:00pm-1:00pm, Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Get ready for Otter Chaos! This furry video game music band is back for another chaotic concert at FurSquared! They perform original arrangements of tunes from game series such as Mario, Sonic, Undertale and Pokemon. You won't want to miss this chaotic show featuring an eclectic group of instruments including accordion, electric violin, trombone, drums, and more!
