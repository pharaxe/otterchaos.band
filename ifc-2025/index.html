Otter Chaos! at IndyFurCon

Sunday, August 17, 2025, 1:00pm-2:30pm, Indianapolis, Indiana
Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Apple Calendar (.ics)

Otter Chaos! is a group of furry musicians who love video game music. We've prepared a selection of songs from our favorite video games for you to enjoy at IndyFurCon! Come and hear live music from games like Baldur's Gate, Fire Emblem, Pokemon, and Sonic the Hedgehog; featuring instruments such as electric violin, steel pans, theremin, accordion and more!

Register for IndyFurCon
